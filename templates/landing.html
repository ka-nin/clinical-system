PATIENT JOURNEY FLOW

Four Stages, One Continuous Digital Record

See how CuraRecord eliminates physical medical folders and paper slips at every touchpoint. Information moves instantly without manual handoffs.

1Reception Station

Digital Registration

Patient registers digitally on a kiosk or tablet. Intake details populate the unified patient record instantly, eliminating paper forms forever.

2Nursing & Triage

Clinical Pre-Check

Nurses record vitals (BP, heart rate, temp, weight) directly into the record. No more paper pre-check slips passed around the clinic.

3Consultation Room

Unified Consultation

Doctors open the exact same patient file, instantly reviewing vitals and clinical history to diagnose and prescribe without folder searching.

4Permanent Storage

Update & Secure Vault

Consultation notes, prescriptions, and lab data are permanently saved to the patient's record for seamless future visits.

SECURITY & PRIVACY

Built for Privacy & Complete Audit Trails

Patient information deserves careful handling. CuraRecord limits access by role and records who did what.

  • Privacy by Design

    Access is limited by role, sessions time out, and every connection is served over HTTPS in production.

  • Granular Role Permissions

    Precisely define what staff, administrators, and patients can read, write, or download—never compromising privacy.

  • Tamper-Evident Audit Trails

    Every record access, edit and change is logged, and the log is chained so that later changes can be detected.
